## Why PostPocket Pro?

**✨ Draft with Confidence**
*   **Offline-First Drafts**: Save your ideas locally instantly. No internet connection required.
*   **Live Twitter Validation**: The native editor actively counts up to 280 characters matching live X URL-shortening metric rules and detects threads! #Hashtags and @Mentions highlight dynamically as you type.
*   **Drag & Drop Flow**: Reorder your scheduled drafts with a modern, beautifully rounded QAbstractItemView interface.

**🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Security**
*   **AES-256 Database Encryption**: Your unpublished drafts are your intellectual property. Enable `Security Mode` to invisibly map 32-byte `cryptography.fernet` symmetric keys to your OS profile. Not even a hex-editor can read your flat-files!
*   **2FA License Protection (v1.1.0)**: Secure your PRO features with Time-Based One-Time Passwords (TOTP).

**⚡ Unfair AI Advantages**
*   **Grok AI Hashtags**: Press one button and let Grok instantly append viral, trending parameters to your draft's metadata. 
*   **OpenAI Ghostwriting**: Highlight any sluggish sentence and instantly rewrite it using OpenAI's GPT models natively built into the Editor toolbar.

**📊 Analytics that Matter**
*   **No-Code Dashboards**: Pull live `fetch_stats_bulk` metrics over your network and sort by Total Likes, Retweets, and Replies in one click. 
*   **Exportable Graphs**: Visualize your growth visually using PySide6 charting maps, instantly exporting into `.png` charts to share with clients or sponsors!
